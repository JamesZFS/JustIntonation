# Salamander Grand Piano

Piano samples by Alexander Holm, from the Salamander Grand Piano library.
Source: https://github.com/tonejs/audio/tree/master/salamander
Downloaded from https://tonejs.github.io/audio/salamander/
License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 (CC BY 3.0)
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

The original recordings are bundled as MP3 samples. The instrument adjusts their playback rate in real time to render exact just-intonation pitches.
